SummaryMicrosoft is retiring SMS first-factor sign-in for Microsoft Entra ID workforce tenants worldwide starting February 1, 2027, to enhance security. Organizations must identify affected users, migrate them to phishing-resistant methods like passkeys, and update policies to avoid sign-in disruptions and comply with new requirements.
